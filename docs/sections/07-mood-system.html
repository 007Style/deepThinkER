07 — Mood System

The characters in deepThinkER are not emotionless text generators. They have emotional states that shift dynamically based on what's happening in the conversation — and those states genuinely influence how they respond, not just how they're labelled.

When SAGE gets Agitated, they don't just get an 😠 icon — the word "agitated" is injected directly into their system prompt, nudging the model toward sharper, more confrontational outputs. When WATSON is Excited, the prompt acknowledges genuine enthusiasm. The MoodEngine connects emotional state to actual inference behaviour.

The Five Mood States

🤩
Excited
Direct address by name, strong agreement, positive citations, being praised
More enthusiastic, verbose, exploratory responses
😊
Engaged
Normal active participation, responding and being responded to
Baseline active mode — focused and constructive
😐
Neutral
Default resting state, minimal recent activity
No special modifier applied to system prompt
😶
Withdrawn
Being ignored for an extended period, low participation
Shorter responses, reduced initiative to volunteer opinions
😠
Agitated
Repeated contradictions, challenge overload, rapid disagreements
Sharper, more confrontational tone. May push back harder.

How It's Implemented

The MoodEngine maintains a continuous float score for each character. On every message event — a character receiving a response, being directly named, being contradicted, being ignored — the score shifts by a small delta. The current score maps to one of the five states above, and that state label is injected into the SystemPromptBuilder output before every inference call.

The score is bounded, decays toward neutral over time, and resets to neutral at the start of each new session. Mood is not persisted between sessions — each conversation starts fresh emotionally, even if memories and relationships carry over.

Emotional Sensitivity by Character

😐 WATSON Most stoic. Hard to move from Neutral or Engaged. Rarely reaches Excited or Agitated.
😊 DEEP Steadily engaged. Patient and resilient. Takes sustained provocation to reach Agitated.
🤩 NOVA High variability. Swings between Excited and Withdrawn quickly. Very responsive to attention.
😠 SAGE Most volatile. Reaches Agitated fastest, cools slowly. Contradiction is both trigger and fuel.
Mood is real, not decorative. The emoji on the quadrant header is just the visible indicator. The actual effect is the mood label being injected into the system prompt before inference — so the model genuinely processes "You are currently feeling agitated" as part of its context. Whether a given model leans into this or ignores it depends on the model, which adds its own layer of unpredictability.
Mood resets each session. Emotional states are ephemeral by design. What carries across sessions are memories and relationships — not current emotional temperature. This reflects the intuition that you can pick up a long-running conversation with someone and feel fresh, even while your history with them remains intact.
