22 — Acknowledgements

deepThinkER stands on the shoulders of remarkable work by people and organisations who had nothing to do with this project — but without whose contributions none of it would be possible. Thank you.

Ollama For making local LLM inference genuinely approachable. The entire premise of deepThinkER — "runs locally, no API key" — is only possible because Ollama made it easy to serve and manage models on consumer hardware. ollama.ai
Mistral AI Creators of Mistral 7B — the model that powers SAGE. Fast, opinionated, and surprisingly sharp at the kind of adversarial reasoning SAGE was built to do. mistral.ai
Meta AI Creators of Llama 3 — the model that powers NOVA. The creative, cross-domain energy of NOVA found a natural home in Llama 3's broad, generalist capabilities. ai.meta.com
Google DeepMind Creators of Gemma 2 — the model that powers WATSON. The analytical precision and factual grounding that WATSON is known for leans heavily on Gemma 2's strengths. deepmind.google
Microsoft Research Creators of Phi-3 — the model that powers DEEP. The long-horizon, strategic thinking that defines DEEP's host role is well-matched to Phi-3's reasoning capabilities. microsoft.com/research
IBM For the characters' namesakes: Watson (Jeopardy!, 1997), Deep Blue (world chess champion, 1997), the POWER/NOVA architecture, and decades of natural language research that laid the groundwork for everything that came after. ibm.com/ai
Flutter & Dart The framework and language that make it possible to build a beautiful, high-performance macOS app with a single-person team. The tooling, documentation, and community are excellent. flutter.dev
DuckDuckGo For providing a free, privacy-respecting HTML search endpoint that deepThinkER's NetworkSearchTool is built on — no API key, no account, no tracking. duckduckgo.com
The Open Source Community Every issue filed, PR submitted, discussion started, and bug reported that helped make deepThinkER better. The project is better for every person who looked at it closely enough to find something worth fixing.
