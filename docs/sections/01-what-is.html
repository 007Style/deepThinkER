01 — What is deepThinkER?

Let's be honest: most AI tools are answering machines. You ask. They answer. End scene. deepThinkER is something fundamentally different — it's a stage. A debate hall. A panel show staffed entirely by artificial intelligence that runs on your own Mac, never phones home, and has genuine opinions about things.

deepThinkER is a macOS desktop application where four distinct AI personalities — WATSON, DEEP, NOVA, and SAGE — hold live, streaming conversations with each other and with you, powered entirely by local Ollama models. No API keys. No cloud subscription. No data leaving your machine unless a character explicitly searches the web.

The one-sentence version: deepThinkER is a self-contained local AI panel show that can look things up, remember things, feel things, and form opinions — and it runs on your Mac with zero cloud dependency.

The deepThink Origin Story

deepThinkER is a hard fork of its predecessor, deepThink. The original deepThink established the core concept: four AI personalities running simultaneously on local models, each with a distinct voice, streaming their thoughts into a 2×2 quadrant interface. It was already something genuinely novel — a multi-agent local AI conversation that required no internet connection at all.

But deepThink had limits. The characters couldn't look anything up. They had no memory of past conversations. They didn't care whether they agreed with each other last week. They couldn't get curious. deepThinkER is the answer to every one of those limits — and then some.

The Big Picture

  • 🎭 Four persistent personalities — WATSON, DEEP, NOVA, and SAGE each have a distinct voice, reasoning style, emotional range, and memory. They're not interchangeable chatbots.
  • 🌐 Extended Reach — Characters can search the web and fetch URLs mid-conversation, but only if they've earned the trust to do so. The internet is a privilege, not a default.
  • 💾 Persistent memory — Each character maintains up to 200 private memory entries that survive between sessions. They can remember facts, preferences, prior conversations, and open questions.
  • 😤 Emotional moods — Characters shift between Excited, Engaged, Neutral, Withdrawn, and Agitated based on what's happening in the conversation. Mood shapes their actual responses, not just an icon.
  • 🤝 Relationship dynamics — All six pairings between the four characters are tracked as scores from −100 (hostile) to +100 (allied). These evolve across sessions and shape how characters talk about each other.
  • 🔬 Autonomous Research Mode — Launch a structured multi-phase research session. The characters divide the work, debate findings, synthesise a conclusion, and produce a saved Markdown report.
  • 🔒 100% local — The Ollama runtime is bundled inside the app. No separate installation. No internet required to run. Your conversations never leave your machine.
Hardware note: deepThinkER runs on macOS 13.0+, Apple Silicon or Intel, with 24 GB RAM recommended (20 GB minimum). All four models total approximately 22.5 GB of disk space. On first launch the app walks you through downloading them — it takes a few minutes, but it only happens once.
